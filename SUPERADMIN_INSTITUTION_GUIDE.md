# Institutional Admin Guide

## Overview
Institutional Admins manage their institution's users, dashboard, and analytics. This guide explains how to log in, access dashboards, and manage institution-specific features.

## Login
- Use your institution-provided email and password.
- If you forget your password, use the "Forgot Password" link on `/auth/login`.

## Accessing Institution Dashboard
1. Log in at `/auth/login`.
2. Navigate to **Institution Dashboard** via the navbar dropdown or `/admin/institution-dashboard`.
3. Manage users at `/admin/institution-users`.

## Key Features
- **Dashboard:** View institution analytics and recent activity.
- **User Management:** Add, edit, or remove institution users.
- **Reports:** Download performance and usage reports.

## Tips
- Keep user data up to date for accurate analytics.
- Contact support@testcraft.in for technical help.
